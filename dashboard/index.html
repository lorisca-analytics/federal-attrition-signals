Who leaves federal agencies first

Quit rates by agency and job type from OPM's public workforce files. Months where an agency's report falls far below its usual level are held out of the ranking, so a reporting gap doesn't read as people staying.

Check first

Separations reported each month

A month fails the check when an agency that usually reports at least 20 separations sends under a quarter of both its median for the previous 12 months and the same month a year earlier. Failing months are ringed, and that agency's fiscal year is left out of every ranking below.
